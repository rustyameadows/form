# Paper and packaging source audit

This is the structural audit record; see the [completed native validation report](paper-packaging-validation-report.md) for subsequent MCP, image and output evidence.

October 30, 2026 · source coverage for the [twelve-case plan](paper-packaging-validation-plan.md)

All twelve examples and the separate liner are ordinary [authored JSON definitions](../examples/paper-packaging/). Their category names occur in example data, not new runtime product generators. `form_construction create` gives them the same parameter controls, canonical artwork, panels and validation lifecycle as other authored constructions.

An immutable, command-line Core helper resolved all 13 sources, opening 0/50/100, and a 5% width increase without launching a window or writing the shared build. All nets/poses resolved and width edits preserved panel IDs. [The compact report](validation/paper-packaging/source-geometry-audit.json) records source hashes, dimensions, checks and the compiled-snapshot identity. A separate private exact-kernel probe also passed the eight declared closed-pose checks for 03 and 04. This does not claim installed bridge, rendering, prepress or physical-prototype acceptance.

## Geometry represented by the sources

`PlanarConstructionDefinition` supplies expression-driven outer polygons, stable-ID physical cutouts, hinge trees, joins, explicit component roots and declared interiors. Each root owns one hinge tree; independent roots share the same parameters and retained flat layout. [Component resolution](../Sources/FormCore/PlanarStructuralFeatures.swift) interpolates flat-to-assembled poses and evaluates declared opening expressions. It does not solve joint forces or automatically assemble arbitrary parts.

Cutouts are finite removed material, including their inner walls, not Canvas decoration. The same [resolved polygons](../Sources/FormCore/PlanarConstruction.swift) drive native compound-path meshes, Canvas clipping/hits, exact profile holes and [print contours](../Sources/FormCore/PaperPrintGeometry.swift). Boundaries are polygonal; sampled circles and chamfers are the geometry actually authored. Current bounds are 64 panels, 32 components, 2048 total contour vertices and 0.01–20 mm uniform stock caliper per source. Separate objects can use different stock. Bonded plies resolve to that source's single total caliper; they do not become loose liners.

Default sizes below are millimeters. Packaging body dimensions describe authored nominal dimensions; the separately listed interior is the declared usable W×H×D. Flat net size is not a usable cavity. P/H/R means panels/hinges/component roots; C/J means cutouts/declared joins.

## Declared checks and appearance

[Interior fit](../Sources/FormCore/PlanarInteriors.swift) compares eight corners of actual oriented native contents bounds against a named component-local rectangle and optional opening cross-section. Reports retain owner/target identity and revisions. This is conservative bounds containment and a lateral opening check, not a continuous insertion path, arbitrary cavity reconstruction or exact contact against every wall/insert. The JSON does not establish a fit merely because a declared interior resolves.

[Exact solid checks](../Sources/FormApp/PlanarSolidValidation.swift) extrude the same outer/inner rings into sharp, centered nominal-stock slabs and apply the resolved poses. Only explicitly named panel pairs and thresholds are checked. Example 03 declares six neck/foot-to-floor conditions; 04 declares one lid/base interference and one positive clearance condition. At default Fold 100/Opening 0, the private native probe measured zero interference for all four mailer pairs, foot/floor contact within 2×10⁻¹⁵ mm, and lid/base clearance 1.0000000000000213 mm. These are measured closed-pose conditions, not guarantees for every intermediate pose. Contact checks are evaluated in the stated assembled pose; a flat blank is not expected to satisfy a retaining-contact condition. Other pairs and intermediate assembly paths remain unproved until explicitly exercised. Join overlap values and anchor checks do not generate adhesive, prove bond area or solve retention.

The source JSONs contain geometry/caliper, not finished artwork, stock profiles, plies or finish assignments. The [MCP fixture](../scripts/validate_paper_packaging.py) adds those using the existing commands. Native bonded-ply slabs have real separate thickness/color. Paper tooth and artwork-driven letterpress/emboss/deboss height fields currently produce surface-normal shading; they do not displace the silhouette or create embossing tools. Foil uses material channels, not a physical foil die. These distinctions must remain visible in production metadata and close-up review.

New registered inside sources retain ordinary editable canonical ownership while their `canvasSourceOnly` presentation flag excludes them from Table counts/captures. Historical nil flags keep their original presentation. Neither switching sides nor enumerating frames creates another physical sheet.

## Findings requiring the installed rounds

The audit corrected one concrete data error: greeting-card 11 initially placed its front behind its back while both outside normals faced inward. Core measurements changed from separation·front-normal=−0.38 mm to+0.38 mm after changing only the authored hinge offset to `-stock`; panel IDs, dimensions and cuts stayed unchanged. A closed native front/back review still belongs in the fixture gate. Example 04 also changed its open presentation: the lid translates by `opening/100*(width+40)` and lowers onto its rim beside the base at 100, retaining identical closed geometry and canonical blanks. The modeled contents should rest intentionally on the base floor (local bottom Y=stock, default 1.8 mm); a zero-clearance fit check records that contact rather than implying an air gap.

The audit also replaced 03's original 5 mm vertical tongue tips with actual child feet folding 90° beneath the floor. The feet occupy Y≈0–1.5 mm and the floor Y≈1.5–3 mm; measured contact confirms flat supporting layers. Preserve an underside view and inspect the insertion sequence and retention on actual stock: zero interference and nominal contact alone do not establish lock strength. In 05, distinct lining/core/cover plies are bonded surface construction, not a separate wrapping pattern or archival pH certification. 07 is intentionally a loose insert; do not describe it as automatically following the closing flap or resizing with its envelope.

The remaining evidence must come from the actual project: reference-linked contents orientation and insert contact; closed/partial/open faces; caliper/ply edits; artwork/finish mask edits; save/reopen and Undo; safe type near inner cuts and glue; registered bleed/cut/crease/finish files; and retained supplier-template bytes/registration. Nonempty renders and successful JSON decoding do not substitute for those checks.

Further capabilities should follow observed prototype needs: curve-defined contours where sampled geometry is insufficient, explicit tongue/hinge material and closure sequencing, cross-object fit/parameter relationships for differently stocked loose parts, and validated tooling/press constraints. The existing data-defined construction path remains the foundation; this audit does not justify a catalog of product-specific runtime code.
