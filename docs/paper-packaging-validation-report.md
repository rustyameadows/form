# Paper and packaging validation report

October 30, 2026 · Twelve editable studies in one LAB project

The twelve studies were created through the native MCP bridge, refined as editable artwork, exported, reopened and reviewed in the Mac renderer. The project contains fifteen physical objects: twelve main studies, two modeled contents objects and one separate envelope liner. Canonical inside-artwork sources remain editable on Canvas without appearing as loose plates on the Table.

## Verified results

The first release MCP verification finished successfully for **12/12 cases**. Its [summary](validation/paper-packaging/mcp-verified-release-summary.json) and [full report](validation/paper-packaging/mcp-verified-release-report.json) retain the actual requests' results and binary/source provenance.

- **74 finite parameter cases passed:** default plus individual width/height/depth endpoints where declared. Sixty are geometry-only passes. Fourteen cases on the locking mailer and presentation box also execute their declared checks, for **56 passed individual solid checks**.
- All twelve retained canonical artwork and finish targets after a width edit, **Undo/Redo/restoration**, and an **exact authored-source reopen**. Every case returned to the same accepted source hash within that run; comparisons exclude only the derived camera viewport aspect.
- **24 invalid stock/finish writes** were rejected without changing source. All ten studies with enabled finishes rejected export after a temporary target deletion; Undo restored the original source.
- **12 production packets** were inspected: **46 SVG process files** share their intended physical extents, **20 PDFs** have checked physical page sizes, and **14 finish plates** retain their assigned artwork targets. The packet tests use **3 mm bleed extents** and sheets **40 mm larger than each net in both axes**; this is an explicit test sheet, not a claim that the net fits a particular commercial press.
- Actual referenced contents passed the declared interior and opening checks for **04 at 0 mm clearance** and **05 at 1 mm clearance**. The presentation box's modeled product rests at its base stock's 1.8 mm floor height.
- The controlled SVG supplier-template fixture retained its original bytes, hash, mapped technical channels and instructions. Registered-original and artwork-only handoffs were inspected, and stale geometry was rejected. The fixture is LAB-authored, not a commercial supplier approval.

The final combined regression gate passed **134 tests with zero failures** in 157.45 seconds using actual AppKit/corelibs XCTest. It includes native controls, fabric compatibility and the new capability roundtrip. The [native regression record](validation/paper-packaging/native-regressions.json) and [complete log](validation/paper-packaging/native-regressions.log) retain the result.

A subsequent focused [template file-version regression](validation/paper-packaging/template-schema-regression.json) also passed all three source-presentation tests, including the additional template-only save/reopen case. ## Corrections and limits

The visual review led to concrete artwork and assembly corrections: the presentation lid moves beside its base and its contents contact the floor; envelope bond branding uses upright surface frames; the liner's unintended large inside title is hidden; tag branding clears the punch; and the contact, postal and greeting reverses contain appropriate content. Existing finish-target IDs were retained. The artwork refinement's second MCP pass planned zero changes. An earlier debug post-batch read timed out while maps refreshed; the accepted batch was preserved, verified by read-only recovery, and followed by a successful idempotency check. The release checker completed without that failure.

The ordinary sheet-proof PDFs for cases [03](validation/paper-packaging/pdf-proof-previews/03.png), [09](validation/paper-packaging/pdf-proof-previews/09.png), [11](validation/paper-packaging/pdf-proof-previews/11.png) and [12](validation/paper-packaging/pdf-proof-previews/12.png) were also rendered to images and visually reviewed. Blind and foil artwork is intentionally absent from ordinary ink proofs and is retained on separate plates.

Stock caliper is authoritative: bonded-ply thicknesses sum to the same construction thickness. Loose liners remain separate objects. Grain, roughness, ink and metallic/relief channels use editable stock/finish declarations and canonical artwork masks. Relief depth/height is recorded as physical process intent, but the current finishing appearance uses normal-map shading; it does **not** generate pressed tooling geometry, change the silhouette, or prove compression or displacement. Bounded texture resolution still limits close-up text and relief fidelity.

The checks establish finite geometry, stated rigid-panel relationships, current-pose oriented contents bounds, source persistence and inspected output structure. Declared solid checks apply at the evaluated poses, not to a sweep of the folding motion. They do not establish a continuous insertion path, exact panel-to-product contact, crease compression, springback, adhesive strength, lock retention, tear behavior or a supplier-approved production specification. Most examples declare no solid manufacturing relationships and are explicitly reported as geometry-only. The template path supports an explicitly mapped SVG; arbitrary supplier PDFs/Illustrator files and universal layer/spot-color preservation are outside this evidence. Stocks, finish depths and print conditions remain prototype assumptions requiring material samples, supplier review and a manufactured proof.
