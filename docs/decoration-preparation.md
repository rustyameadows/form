# HTV and DTF decoration preparation

Milestone 38 prepares local physical handoff files from a finite artwork frame or a garment decoration. It does not operate equipment. The working subset is opaque, flat vector artwork: shared text/glyph/list outlines, expanded strokes, boolean geometry, transformed groups and nested geometric masks. Unsupported images, gradients, partial transparency, effects and blends produce explicit preflight errors.

## Native workflow

Select a physical artwork frame or an attached decoration and choose **Prepare HTV / DTF…** in its inspector. Choose HTV or DTF, review the physical millimeter dimensions and actual garment zone, then compare **Application** and **Delivery** orientation. Application is unmirrored; the delivery mirror is applied exactly once to the exported geometry. Changing delivery choices does not move the garment attachment or camera.

A 34 merchandise attachment records its actual host, bounded zone, offset and rotation. Tee/tote planar zones and the cap's curved concept band use their existing validation. A cap's flat width is its recorded arc length; neither a curved mockup nor a valid envelope establishes press contact, fabric compensation or material compatibility. Resize the original object with its existing controls before preparing a new revision.

Choose source-background inclusion explicitly. A garment-colored background rectangle is printable artwork unless deliberately excluded. White/show-through and tint-zero exclusions clear earlier paint in those regions; they do not reveal older printable content.

Numeric draft fields retain the text being edited and parse it synchronously when **Capture immutable preparation** is pressed. Invalid, nonfinite or out-of-range values show an error and preserve project/history. There is no implicit clamping or default substitution. The persistent capture button remains visible below the scrollable controls. **Done** discards uncaptured draft options. Assigning a CMYK profile is a separate explicit existing document operation with its own Undo.

Capture saves the current source and exact generated bytes as one shared Undo step. The **Saved preparations** tab reviews its immutable PDF and captured settings, with the existing dedicated physical-document zoom/page navigation. Positions are scoped to project, immutable job ID and document kind. **Production jobs → HTV / DTF preparations** also accesses archives after the original live object has been deleted. Export does not regenerate them.

## HTV cut files

HTV defaults to mirrored, conventional carrier-side delivery. The operator must confirm the chosen product's actual orientation. Each visible material region exports `Cut-01.svg`, `Cut-02.svg`, etc., with real millimeter dimensions, closed compound outlines, outlined text and no duplicate interior boundaries from overlapping same-material shapes. Metadata identifies the region/material and recorded choices. An optional separate weed rectangle expands each page by twice the declared 0–50 mm padding without scaling artwork.

Regions are computed from the final visible artwork in painter order, then unioned by paint identity. Interleaved A/B/A layers, holes, nested clips and tint-zero clears therefore preserve actual knockout geometry. Source process/spot identities do not identify a matching vinyl. Material name, supplier, SKU and notes are explicit declarations; the native first step offers a name field, with richer declarations available through MCP. Fractional named-ink tints cannot silently become solid material silhouettes and reject preflight.

No cutter language, blade pressure, speed, tacking order, overlap, choke or thermal recipe is generated. Thin-detail/product limitations remain provider/material decisions.

## DTF handoff

DTF defaults to unmirrored delivery, 300 requested DPI, transparent background, opaque-white printing and optional underbase references. Mirror, source background and white-versus-garment-show-through remain reviewable. The operator must confirm the provider's preferred orientation and file/color requirements.

- **sRGB RGBA PNG** writes `Color.png` with transparency and physical density metadata. Integer pixels round upward; the report records requested DPI and actual encoded X/Y density. Physical dimensions are retained within the encoded density precision. This is an appearance handoff for a vendor/RIP, not a named-ink/device-channel file.
- **Profiled process-CMYK PDF** writes `Color-Process.pdf` with actual ICCBased process colorants and the chosen portable profile. `Reference-color.png` is separately classified as reference. Used declarations must match the explicit output profile. Named spots require explicit process-alternate conversion in the delivery copy; original names, tints, raw components and ICC bytes remain in the immutable source packet.
- `Reference-underbase.svg` and `Reference-underbase.png` contain the final visible silhouette after masks, holes, layer order and white/tint-zero decisions. They are **reference files**, not a generated RIP white channel, density/choke/trap setting or screened plate.

The first subset rejects unsupported paints instead of flattening undeclared color intent. RGB previews do not establish physical ink equivalence. No PDF/X, vendor acceptance, calibrated color or press-result claim is made.

## Immutable packet and source boundaries

A new optional `Project.decorationPreparations` entry requires schema 38. Projects without entries keep their existing minimum schema. Reader support extends through 38; no new tool is registered.

Small project metadata contains identity, quantity, process, captured physical bounds and source/revision digests. Larger original sources, profiles and generated files are SHA-256-addressed under `Production/Decorations/<digest>.asset`. A packet contains:

- `Source.json`: original reachable source owners, component definitions, linked owners, one global rich story, styles, original words/settings/glyph assignments, referenced palette/ink declarations, material/recipe records and target attachment. It also records the physical resolved face without serializing transient story fragments.
- Original recoverable import/image files and exact ICC assets in `Assets/` when referenced by captured owners. Fonts are not bundled or installed.
- Physical delivery files and separately classified references.
- `Review.pdf`: an actual-size artwork page followed by readable A4 specification pages with quantity, explicit options, material/equipment declarations, target placement, notes, preflight findings and scope.
- `Revision.json`, `Job.json` and `Manifest.json`: immutable revision metadata, exact file byte counts/SHA-256, delivery/reference/source classification and packet digest.

Local export produces a new folder or an atomic ZIP. Export verifies archived bytes before touching the destination. Missing/corrupt assets reject without replacing an existing file. Later live edits, font installations, source deletion or Studio changes never alter captured bytes. `current_source` compares original editable records; it does not claim the same fonts are installed. ZIP transport timestamps may differ; packet contents and content digests remain exact. Removing job metadata is undoable and retains immutable assets for Undo recovery.

Capture/export neither reserves nor deducts inventory. No network, printer, cutter or RIP is contacted.

## MCP

The existing `form_production_job` keeps embroidery as its unchanged omitted-kind/default behavior. `kind:"decoration"` supports `list`, `preflight`, `create`, `get`, `remove` and `export`; `event` remains embroidery-only. Preparation dimensions come from the actual object, with no automatic rescaling.

```json
{
  "kind": "decoration",
  "command": "preflight",
  "object_id": "ARTWORK-OBJECT-UUID",
  "preparation": {
    "process": "htv",
    "mirror": true,
    "includeBackground": false,
    "weedMargin": 4.25,
    "layerMaterials": [
      {"paintKey": "rgb:#15324B", "name": "Operator selected navy vinyl", "supplier": "Recorded supplier", "sku": "Recorded SKU"}
    ]
  },
  "quantity": 12
}
```

Use `create` with the same explicit inputs to capture, and inspect returned `job.id`. DTF options include `dpi` (72–600), `whitePolicy:"printOpaqueWhite"|"showThrough"`, `colorDelivery:"sRGBRaster"|"profiledProcess"`, `convertSpotAlternates` and `includeUnderbaseReference`. Optional `materialNotes` and `equipmentNotes` remain declarations. `get` accepts `include_source:true` up to 1 MB; export the packet for larger original records. `export` takes `job_id`, `format:"zip"|"folder"` and an optional absolute `path`. Archived export/read preserves a current native text draft and selection. Production capture is deliberately excluded from `form_batch`; invalid batches reject before capture.

## Bounds and evidence

A preparation permits 256 reachable original objects, 4,096 vector paints, 250,000 source/clip segments and at most 64 visible HTV material regions. Raster output is capped at 64 megapixels and 10,000 pixels per edge; generated files are capped at 64 MB each and packets at 128 MB. Source, declaration, notes and revision counts are bounded. Preview draws fitted vectors, rebuilds only on explicit preview-affecting changes/refresh, and does not allocate full-DPI images while typing. Archive rows and PDF loading are lazy; the shared document viewer retains at most 24 scalar viewports.

The isolated source checkpoint passes **680 graphics/IPC-enabled tests** (479 core/graphics + 201 native app), including 19 new decoration regressions and the accepted focused ink-Save fixes. It is based on the prior 657-test ink tree plus those four fixes; it is not a claim that newer main changes have been integrated. New tests cover real 34 zones/NASA art, normal knockout/counters/nested masks, physical mirror/weed output, alpha clears, actual CMYK, original source/profile/import preservation, shared story/component ownership, one-step Undo/autosave/reopen, source deletion, corrupt destination protection and actual native capture-button actions with an active numeric field. Twelve real merchandise/NASA HTV plan builds measure **12.52 ms median / 14.42 ms p95** on the validation machine; this is a bounded vector-preparation measurement, not cutter/RIP throughput.

`verify_decoration_handoff.py` independently checks exported packet hashes, classification and SVG/PNG physical dimensions using Python's standard library. Poppler independently confirms the physical review/A4 boxes and renders the specification; Quick Look renders the actual cut SVG; actual RGBA and native preparation views are visually reviewed. Physical material application remains unverified.

The integrated release passes **719 tests** (497 core/graphics/IPC and 222 native), with zero failures/skips. The guarded packaged workflow passes HTV, RGBA DTF and actual profiled-process delivery, original spot/ICC preservation, exact Undo/Redo, source deletion/reopen, and immutable folder/ZIP exports. Actual native capture stores focused **127.5 DPI** and **4.25 mm** weed padding before any bridge read, retaining the selected material declaration, source and camera. Each capture is one exact native Undo; Redo restores the same revision. Invalid −5 DPI rejects visibly without changing the project, and a later HTV capture ignores the inactive DTF-only draft.

Actual native archive review is sharp at 400%; all four mouse pan directions reverse exactly. Switching between captured revisions restores each revision's own page/zoom/position. A native Save dialog exports a packet independently verified by hashes and physical density: the 96 × 30 mm DTF sample encodes 482 × 151 RGBA pixels with explicit X/Y density; the padded HTV cut is 104.5 × 38.5 mm. Studio's project-wide **Production jobs → HTV / DTF preparations** opens the exact archive even after its live object is deleted. Actual process restart and native Home reopen preserve both captured preparations and all project source/history/camera. Native visual review also caught and fixed a preview intrinsic-height layout overflow before release. The ordinary five-project library and Studio settings remain unchanged. ## Primary guidance and scope

- [Siser's HTV tips](https://www.siserna.com/top-ten-siser-heat-transfer-vinyl-tips/) inform the explicit conventional carrier-side mirror default. This is not a default for every printable product.
- [STAHLS UltraColor MAX artwork specification](https://assets.stahls.com/stahls/content/pdf/ultracolor-max/UltraColorMax-ArtSpecs.pdf) informs actual-size artwork, outlined text/strokes, transparent PNG/vector PDF and its recommended 300 DPI. Product-specific dimensions/detail limits and formats are not universal compatibility presets.
- [Transfer Express artwork guidance](https://blog.transferexpress.com/art-guidelines/) informs explicit process-CMYK versus RGB delivery; its [product description](https://www.transferexpress.com/product_detail/displayOrder?codeName=ultracolor_max) informs white-versus-show-through review. An alternate preview is not a measured physical ink match.
- [Epson's DTF guide](https://files.support.epson.com/docid/other/cmp0437-00_en.pdf) treats white/film/RIP and fixing conditions as equipment/media-specific. FORM does not invent a thermal recipe or label a silhouette as an equipment white channel.

Broader raster/gradient/transparency handoff, product-specific production settings, verified underbase/channel preparation, purchasing/cost/inventory integration and physical compatibility remain separate open requirements in the governing textile brief.
