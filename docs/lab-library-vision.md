# Lab library

This document records the product direction established for a unified Lab library.

- **One unified area in the app.** Bring materials, equipment, and related libraries together in one place, where people can see both their overall Lab setup and the resources for a particular project.

- **Lab and project resources have distinct scopes.** An item added to the Lab library belongs to the overall setup. An item added to a project belongs to that project.

- **Materials are for designing.** People should be able to add and use materials whether or not they physically have them. The library can include 3D printer filaments, papers, cloths, fabrics, and colors.

- **The experience should be visual.** Materials should be represented through swatches and other visual previews, rather than primarily as lists. Filament should show its color; fabrics and other materials should convey their texture and materiality.

- **Machines should be visually represented.** Devices should have appealing 3D renderings, similar in spirit to Apple’s printer illustrations.

- **Equipment belongs to the overall setup.** People can define their devices in the Lab, since those generally carry across projects.

- **Device integrations should be broad where possible.** For equipment that supports it, the Lab could show information such as device status and supply levels.

- **Other resources may belong in the unified area.** Fonts, design assets, and projects are possibilities to consider as the scope takes shape.

- **Build on what already exists.** The app already has parts of a shared library for materials, filament, equipment, and thread stock. This work is about bringing the experience together and making it more visual.
