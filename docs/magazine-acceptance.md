# Publication acceptance: VARIOUS quarterly

This is the final acceptance case for the multi-sheet publication work. The
12-page booklet and 64-page hardcover remain regression examples; neither is a
substitute for this magazine.

## Physical publication

- Finished trim: 8.5 × 11 inches (215.9 × 279.4 mm).
- 48 editorial interior pages plus four cover pages: 52 logical pages.
- 13 folded parent sheets, each 17 × 11 inches (431.8 × 279.4 mm), duplex.
- Print PDF: 26 landscape sheet sides, exact sheet dimensions, no added bleed.
  Artwork uses an inset safe area because the two trims already fill the sheet.
- Reader layout: front cover, 25 facing pairs, back cover: 27 reader spreads.
  Print signatures and reader spreads are separate derived arrangements.
- Uncoated stocks. Four colored parent sheets carry black-only ink; nine white
  parent sheets carry full-color work. Both halves and both sides of a parent
  share the same authoritative stock. Substrate color is a preview/material,
  never an accidentally printed background rectangle.
- Two adjustable binding choices: saddle stitching through the fold, and side
  staples through the bound margin. Both must be visibly represented.

The parent-sheet plan uses colored sheets 0, 4, 8 and 12 (zero based), giving
16 colored page faces and 36 white faces. The outer sheet is the four covers.
The physical model must derive viewing leaves from these parents without
maintaining a second editable stock assignment.

## Editorial issue

Use an original demonstration issue, with original artwork and clearly identified
fictional artist/studio profiles. Do not invent claims about real artists. Record
asset provenance and reuse. Aim for approximately 6,200 words and 100 meaningful
visual placements, with imagery carrying the issue rather than repeated filler.

## Canvas design acceptance

1. Setting up/opening a facing publication presents editable reader spreads by
   default, independently of whether its navigation rail is open.
2. Show physical page edges, a gutter and whole-spread navigation/focus. Preserve
   ordinary nonpublication Canvas behavior.
3. An image or text element can cross the gutter and be picked, moved and resized
   from either half. It has exactly one canonical owner; the opposite page is a
   derived crop, never a second editable copy.
4. Source edits, text flow, masters, numbering, Undo/Redo and reopen remain exact.
   Verify both exported page crops and the corresponding 3D faces after changes.
5. Colored stock is visible while designing, while black-only print sources
   remain actual black ink and transparent substrate.

## Studio acceptance

- Correct cover/inside artwork, stock color, uncoated surface and physical caliper.
- Opening control from fully closed through 180 degrees, plus visible spread.
- The model responds coherently throughout; covers, stacks and binding follow.
- Independent instances of the same publication can show different openings and
  spreads, sharing the original artwork/materials. Save and reopen one scene
  containing at least three different open spreads and a closed copy.
- Show both binding styles and materially different stocks. Inspect front, back,
  spine, foreedge and printed detail. Fix visible rendering artifacts rather than
  accepting geometry validation as visual proof.

## Required evidence

Retain actual native edits, successive render rounds (including rejected ones),
all-page contact sheets and detail views, source/export hashes and public-tool
requests. Verify all 52 reader pages, all 27 reader spreads and all 26 imposed
sheet sides, page identities, stock grouping, black-only channels and exact PDF
boxes. Test native controls, instance independence, latest-result behavior,
Undo/reopen, and existing paper/fabric/CAD compatibility. Commit the reproducible
example data and compact accepted evidence with the implementation PR.

Digital layout and appearance evidence does not establish calibrated press output,
mechanical binding strength, printer margin capability or a manufactured proof.
No printer, motion or heating commands are part of this acceptance run.
